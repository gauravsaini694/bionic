“Do I really need to go back?” We get it, after your first visit you might feel great and think, “Everything’s fine, why go back?”

But here’s the truth: Follow-ups aren’t just for when something goes wrong.

They’re for making sure you stay on track, prevent problems before they start, and get the best results from your treatment or device.

Think of it like a car service. You wouldn’t wait until your car completely breaks down to check it, right?

## Why Follow-Ups Matter

Your body changes over time and so do your needs.Follow-ups help us:

- Spot issues early before they become major problems
- Adjust your device so it fits and works perfectly
- Track your progress and celebrate wins
- Answer your questions and give expert guidance

Skipping follow-up appointments? That’s like skipping half the recipe and expecting the dish to taste the same.

## When You Should Come Back

### 1️. Routine Check-Ups

Even if everything feels fine, it’s smart to come in every few months (or as your specialist recommends).We will make sure everything is still fitting, working, and supporting you the way it should.

Pro tip: Mark your calendar after each visit so you never miss the next one.

### 2️. Something Feels Off

- Your device feels uncomfortable
- You notice redness, swelling, or skin irritation
- It’s harder to walk, move, or do daily activities

### 3️. Life Changes

Weight changes, surgery, new activity levels, or even a growth spurt in kids can mean your device needs adjustment.Your body is telling a new story and your care should adapt to it.

### 4️. You Have Questions or Concerns

Even if it feels “too small” to mention, we want to hear it. Maybe all it takes is a phone call.

Your peace of mind is part of your care.

## What Happens in a Follow-Up?

- Comfort check: We make sure you feel good using your device
- Performance check: Testing and adjustments for the best function
- Education: Tips to keep things in great shape between visits
- Plan ahead: Setting your next check-up date

## Bottom Line:

Don’t wait for pain, discomfort, or problems.Follow-ups are like your health’s pit stop: quick, essential, and the best way to keep you moving forward.
