Many patients hesitate to schedule follow-up appointments, assuming they're only necessary when something goes wrong. But follow-ups aren't just for when something goes wrong — think of them like routine maintenance for your car: skipping them doesn't save you trouble in the long run, it just delays the discovery of small issues before they become big ones.

## Why Follow-Ups Matter

Regular follow-up visits offer several key benefits:

- Spotting issues early, before they become painful or limit mobility
- Adjusting the device for optimal fit as your body changes
- Tracking your progress over time
- Providing expert guidance tailored to your specific needs

Bodies change over time — through weight fluctuations, activity level changes, muscle development, and more — making ongoing professional support essential to keeping your prosthesis working the way it should.

## When to Schedule Follow-Ups

1. **Routine check-ups** — Appointments every few months even when you're feeling fine.
2. **When something feels off** — Including discomfort, skin irritation, or reduced mobility.
3. **Life changes** — Weight fluctuations, surgery, new activities, or growth in children.
4. **Questions or concerns** — Direct communication about any uncertainties with your device or your care.

## What Happens During Visits

A typical follow-up includes a comfort assessment, performance testing with any needed adjustments, educational guidance on device maintenance, and scheduling for future appointments.

Follow-ups are like your health's pit stop: quick, essential, and the best way to keep you moving forward.
