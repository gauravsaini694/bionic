At Bionic, we are thrilled to announce that one of our esteemed Regional Managers and Certified Prosthesis & Orthotists, L. Bradley Watson, has been elected to the board of directors of the National Association for the Advancement of Orthotics and Prosthetics (NAAOP). This prestigious appointment highlights Brad\'s dedication to our profession and his wealth of experience accumulated over 35 years.

Ann Leimkuehler Moss, NAAOP\'s board president, expressed her delight at welcoming Brad to the board. She emphasized Brad\'s extensive experience, including his previous and current roles on the BOC board and the Kentucky state licensure board. These roles underscore Brad\'s unwavering commitment to advancing the field of orthotics and prosthetics.

At Bionic, we share in the excitement of Brad\'s appointment and recognize the significance of his contributions to the NAAOP board. Brad\'s appointment aligns perfectly with our core values and mission to provide exceptional care and support to individuals with limb loss and limb differences.

![Bionic's Brad Watson Named to NAAOP Board of Directors image 1](/images/blog/inline/bionic-s-brad-watson-named-to-naaop-board-of-directors/1.jpg)

As an organization, Bionic is committed to staying at the forefront of advancements in orthotics and prosthetics. Brad\'s involvement with NAAOP further solidifies our dedication to promoting public policy that supports the interests of the O&P community and drives the advancement of our field.

Brad\'s extensive expertise and leadership will undoubtedly enhance the NAAOP board\'s efforts to advocate for policies that benefit individuals with limb loss and limb differences. His passion for improving the lives of our patients aligns seamlessly with Bionic\'s mission to empower individuals to achieve their full potential through education, support, and advocacy.

We are proud to have Brad Watson as part of the Bionic team and congratulate him on this well-deserved appointment to the NAAOP board of directors. His continued commitment to excellence and innovation inspires us all as we work together to transform the lives of those we serve.
