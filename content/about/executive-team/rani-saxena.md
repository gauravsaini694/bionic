Rani Saxena is the President of Bionic Orthotics & Prosthetics, a forward-focused leader known for her ability to align clinical excellence with strategic growth. With a Doctorate in Physical Therapy and extensive experience in physical rehabilitation and prosthetic management, she brings both clinical depth and business insight to the evolving field of rehabilitative care.

Rani’s leadership is defined by empathy, accountability, and results. She has built and led multidisciplinary teams across clinical, technical, and administrative functions, fostering a culture of collaboration, high performance, and patient-centered care. Her ability to unify staff around a shared mission has been instrumental in expanding operations across regions, integrating new technologies, and achieving consistently high patient satisfaction.

With a strong command of project management, Rani has successfully overseen complex initiatives ranging from digital transformation and compliance upgrades to clinic expansion and payor contracting. Her experience includes optimizing workflows, leading team integration, launching quality improvement programs, and building scalable systems that support long-term growth.

Beyond clinical leadership, Rani possesses a deep understanding of business operations in healthcare. She actively engages in strategic planning, financial oversight, marketing direction, and partnership development with hospitals, rehab centers, and referring providers. Her business-savvy mindset ensures that patient care never comes at the expense of efficiency or sustainability.

Balancing her professional drive with a rich personal life, Rani is a proud mother of two and a dog. She believes leadership begins with listening, and she leads with compassion, clarity, and conviction—building not just a company, but a purpose-driven community committed to restoring mobility and dignity to those they serve.
