      - question: "How do I schedule an appointment?"
        answer: "Call us! We are always more than happy to help — reach our team at 1-855-524-6642."
      - question: "Is the initial consultation free?"
        answer: "Yes! Make sure you stop by and see us today."
      - question: "Are prescriptions required?"
        answer: "A prescription is not required for a consultation. However, a prescription is required in order for us to provide a device to a patient. We strongly recommend each patient obtain a prescription prior to their initial visit — if this isn't possible, Bionic will be happy to help with the process, and we'll provide a written plan outlining our course of treatment at no charge."
      - question: "Do you charge for an office visit?"
        answer: "We do not charge for routine office visits. Routine visits and adjustments are covered under the original charge for the orthotic or prosthetic device. Non-routine adjustments or repairs may be subject to a fee if the initial device warranty is no longer in effect."
      - question: "How can I get a device if I can't make it into one of your clinics?"
        answer: "If you're not able to make the trip to one of our offices, we can arrange for one of our specialists to provide services at home, or at a nursing or assisted living facility, at no charge."
      - question: "Does my insurance cover my prosthesis?"
        answer: "While most medical insurance coverage will include prosthetic limbs with a prescription from your doctor, it's best to check your policy and call your insurance company to confirm coverage."
      - question: "How often do I have to come in to get my prosthesis adjusted?"
        answer: "As much as you feel necessary! There's no limit to the number of visits a patient can request — we're here to help."
