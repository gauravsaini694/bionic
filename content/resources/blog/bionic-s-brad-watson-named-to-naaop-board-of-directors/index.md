L. Bradley Watson, a Regional Manager and Certified Prosthetist & Orthotist at Bionic, has been elected to the board of directors of the National Association for the Advancement of Orthotics and Prosthetics (NAAOP). Watson brings more than 35 years of experience in the field to this new role.

NAAOP Board President Ann Leimkuehler Moss welcomed Watson to the board, noting his extensive background, including his previous and current roles on the BOC board and the Kentucky state licensure board.

Bionic celebrates Watson's appointment as a reflection of the organization's ongoing commitment to advancing the fields of orthotics and prosthetics. "Brad's involvement with NAAOP further solidifies our dedication to promoting public policy" that benefits the broader O&P community. Watson's expertise and leadership are expected to strengthen the board's advocacy efforts on behalf of individuals with limb loss and limb differences, aligning closely with Bionic's mission to empower patients through education, support, and innovation.
