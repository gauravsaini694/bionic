## Key Statistics

More than 500 individuals in the United States experience limb loss every day, and projections suggest the population living with limb loss could nearly double by 2050. Today, more than 2.7 million people across the country are navigating life with limb loss or limb difference, and this reality touches individuals across every demographic.

## About LLLDAM

Limb Loss and Limb Difference Awareness Month was established in 2011 by the Amputee Coalition and takes place every April. The initiative aims to amplify voices, giving affected individuals the space to share their personal journeys while advancing education and advocacy for the broader community.

## Why Support Matters

Collective action matters. Individual contributions — whether a letter to an elected official or a modest donation — add up to create meaningful change for people seeking assistive devices and better access to the services they need.

## Three Ways to Support

**Social Media Advocacy:** Share LLLDAM ribbons and information across your platforms using resources from the Amputee Coalition's communications toolkit.

**Strategic Shopping:** Purchase from the Amputee Coalition store or from disability-owned businesses to help fund research and advocacy initiatives.

**Mental Health Support:** Approximately 36 percent of individuals with limb loss experience depression. Take time to explore the Amputee Coalition's emotional recovery resources, and share them with someone who might need them.
