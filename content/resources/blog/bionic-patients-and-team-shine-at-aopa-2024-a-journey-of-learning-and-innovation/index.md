In September, members of the Bionic team attended the American Orthotic & Prosthetic Association (AOPA) Conference in Charlotte, North Carolina. The event provided educational courses, product demonstrations, and networking opportunities for the clinical team to enhance their expertise and learn about recent developments in the orthotics and prosthetics field.

Two long-time Bionic patients, Reynaldo Morales and Raymond Conley, both above-knee amputees, participated in the conference for the first time. This marked the first instance of Bionic patients attending an O&P conference alongside the company's clinical team.

Morales commented on the experience: "The event was a learning experience on so many levels. I have a whole new level of appreciation for the fabrication process."

Conley shared similar impressions: "It opened my eyes to what all is out there, especially with regards to highly advanced technology. The team was also very relatable and had an awesome positive energy."

Bionic exhibited its One Source platform at the conference, designed to streamline orthotics and prosthetics practices through features for documentation, coding, purchasing, fabrication, and cost management. The company also introduced newly released prosthetic knees from BrainRobotics and BionicM, showcasing innovations in prosthetic mobility technology.

Bringing patients into the conference experience alongside clinicians reflects Bionic's continued commitment to education, transparency, and innovation across every level of care.
