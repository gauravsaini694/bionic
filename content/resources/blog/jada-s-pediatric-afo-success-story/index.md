## Small Steps, Big Confidence: How an AFO Helped Jada Find Her Rhythm

Jada initially struggled with walking and balance. With the steady support of her foster mother, Dana, she found her way to Bionic Prosthetics & Orthotics' Joliet clinic. Rather than searching for a quick fix, the family pursued meaningful, ongoing support through orthotic care.

## When Walking Feels Like Work

For some children, movement takes conscious effort, and balance has to be learned intentionally rather than developing on its own. As our team puts it, "the goal wasn't to change who she was. It was to support how she moved." Jada's care team recommended a custom AFO (Ankle-Foot Orthosis) — not as a restriction, but as a guide, designed to support proper alignment and reduce physical strain with every step.

## More Than a Brace, A Foundation

Jada's custom-made AFO was built around her individual needs and growth patterns. Progress showed up gradually: longer walks without needing to stop, improved standing balance, and a more natural posture overall.

## The Moment Confidence Takes Over

As movement became easier, Jada's confidence grew right along with it. She began running, jumping, and riding her bike. The turning point came when she danced at her school's Homecoming event, fully participating in the celebration while wearing her AFO.

## Why Early Orthotic Support Matters

Early AFO intervention can improve alignment, reduce muscle strain, make movement easier, and build confidence during important developmental years. Care doesn't stop at the first fitting — it includes ongoing follow-ups and adjustments as children grow.

## A Community That Believes in Small Wins

Jada's story is a reminder that pediatric orthotic care is about more than the device itself. It's about the whole child and the caregivers who support them, recognizing that comfort matters just as much as correction.

## Big Futures Are Built on Small Steps

Jada's AFO doesn't define her — it supports her, giving her the foundation she needs to keep growing into her full potential.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is an AFO?**
An Ankle-Foot Orthosis is a custom brace that supports the foot and ankle to improve stability, alignment, and walking efficiency in children.

**How does an AFO help children walk more confidently?**
The device guides the foot into a stable position, reducing wobbling, improving balance, and lowering the effort required to walk.

**Will an AFO limit my child's movement or play?**
No. A properly fitted AFO supports movement, allowing children to run, jump, and take part fully in school and play activities.

**When should a child be evaluated for an AFO?**
Evaluation is recommended if a child struggles with balance, tires easily, trips frequently, or avoids physical activity.

**How long does a child usually need to wear an AFO?**
Duration varies by child. Regular follow-up appointments allow the device to be adjusted as the child's needs change with growth.

**Are AFOs custom-made for each child?**
Yes — each AFO is individually designed based on the child's anatomy, movement patterns, and growth needs.

**Can children wear regular shoes with an AFO?**
In most cases, yes. Many AFOs are designed to fit inside everyday footwear.

**How do I know if an AFO is working?**
Parents typically notice steadier steps, longer playtime, improved posture, and reduced fatigue.

**Why is early AFO support important?**
Early use helps guide healthy movement patterns, reduces muscle strain, and supports a child's confidence as they develop.
