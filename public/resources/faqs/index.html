FAQS

Answers to Common Prosthetic & Orthotic Questions

How do I schedule an appointment?

Call us! We are always more than happy to help — reach our team at 1-855-524-6642.

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes! Make sure you stop by and see us today.

Are prescriptions required?

A prescription is not required for a consultation. However, a prescription is required in order for us to provide a device to a patient. We strongly recommend each patient obtain a prescription prior to their initial visit — if this isn't possible, Bionic will be happy to help with the process, and we'll provide a written plan outlining our course of treatment at no charge.

Do you charge for an office visit?

We do not charge for routine office visits. Routine visits and adjustments are covered under the original charge for the orthotic or prosthetic device. Non-routine adjustments or repairs may be subject to a fee if the initial device warranty is no longer in effect.

How can I get a device if I can't make it into one of your clinics?

If you're not able to make the trip to one of our offices, we can arrange for one of our specialists to provide services at home, or at a nursing or assisted living facility, at no charge.

Does my insurance cover my prosthesis?

While most medical insurance coverage will include prosthetic limbs with a prescription from your doctor, it's best to check your policy and call your insurance company to confirm coverage.

How often do I have to come in to get my prosthesis adjusted?

As much as you feel necessary! There's no limit to the number of visits a patient can request — we're here to help.

Still Have Questions?

Our team is happy to walk you through your options. Reach out anytime — we're here to help.
