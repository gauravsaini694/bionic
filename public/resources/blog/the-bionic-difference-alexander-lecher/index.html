The Bionic Difference: Alexander Lecher

Success Stories May 13, 2022
Back to Blog

Alexander Lecher is an unstoppable young child who loves playing with stuffed animals, riding his balance bike, exploring outdoors, and visiting playgrounds — despite having a full foot prosthesis.

Macrodactyly & Syme’s Amputation

Alexander was born with macrodactyly, a congenital condition causing abnormal overgrowth of bones and soft tissue in fingers or toes. Pain from this condition necessitated a full-foot amputation in April 2020 when he was just two years old. The procedure, called a Syme’s amputation, involves removing all foot bones and transferring the heel pad to support the leg bones, preserving leg structure while enabling weight-bearing and comfort.

Alexander Receives His First Prosthesis

By July 2020, Alexander’s family visited Bionic Prosthetics & Orthotics and met prosthetist Sheri Harper, CPO. His mother notes that “when Alexander received his first prosthesis — an insanely adorable horse prosthesis — I knew right away that he needed something different.” After researching silicone alternatives with other families, Katie Lecher asked Harper to explore this option, which she enthusiastically pursued.

Going to Any Length for Her Patient

To create the proper silicone device, Harper and technician James Bitter traveled to Spokane, Washington, to study silicone applications. Upon returning, they designed Alexander’s first in-house silicone full-foot prosthesis in Cincinnati, Ohio. His mother reports that “once we received our first silicone prosthesis, it didn’t take him any time at all to adjust.”

An Unstoppable Future and a Bright Smile

Alexander has been fitted with multiple prostheses as he grows, most recently a green silicone version. At four years old, his mother shares that “he really wants to ride a horse” and believes nothing will prevent him from achieving his goals. The family expresses deep gratitude to the Bionic team for their care and dedication.
