      TRUSTED CARE SINCE 2007

      Bionic Prosthetics and Orthotics

      Founded in 2007, Bionic Prosthetics & Orthotics is a nationally recognized provider of personalized prosthetic and orthotic care, serving patients through clinical locations across the country.

      We are a fully accredited orthotic and prosthetic facility, committed to delivering care that meets the highest clinical and professional standards. Our team includes certified and state-licensed prosthetists and orthotists with experience across a wide range of specialties, including upper and lower limb prosthetics, spinal and extremity orthotics, pediatric care, and functional electrical stimulation (FES).

      At Bionic, we believe quality care begins with listening. Every patient’s needs, goals, and daily life are different, and our approach reflects that. From evaluation through long-term follow-up, we focus on providing thoughtful, personalized solutions designed to support comfort, function, and confidence.

      We are here to provide competent, compassionate care every step of the way.

      Care That Adapts to Your Life

      Our Mission

      Our mission is to provide advanced, personalized prosthetic and orthotic care that supports comfort, function, and confidence in everyday life. We strive to deliver clinical excellence through thoughtful solutions and compassionate patient care.

      Our Vision

      Our vision is to build a collaborative community of dedicated clinicians and partners who share a commitment to improving mobility and quality of life. By expanding our clinical presence nationally and internationally, we aim to strengthen partnerships across the healthcare continuum and elevate the standard of prosthetic and orthotic care.

      Our Purpose

      We are committed to creating awareness and access to high-quality prosthetic and orthotic care, becoming a trusted and active part of the communities we serve. Our goal is to support patients, families, and caregivers through education, engagement, and long-term care relationships.

      WHAT DRIVES US

      Our Values

      Compassion - We listen first and treat every patient with respect, empathy, and understanding.
      Clinical Excellence - We uphold the highest standards of care through expertise, precision, and ongoing education.
      Integrity - We act with honesty, transparency, and accountability in everything we do.
      Community - We believe strong relationships and local engagement are essential to meaningful care.
      Commitment to Patients - We are dedicated to improving lives through competent, compassionate, and personalized care.

      Our Commitment to Community

      We aim to be more than a clinic; we strive to be a trusted partner within every community we serve. By creating awareness around prosthetic and orthotic care, supporting local needs, and fostering connection, we work toward a world where mobility challenges don’t limit a person’s potential.
