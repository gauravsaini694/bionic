      OUR CLINICIANS

      Meet Our Clinical Team

      A nationwide team of board-certified prosthetists and orthotists dedicated to personalized, ongoing patient care.

      Board-certified prosthetists, orthotists and fitters

      EXPERIENCE YOU CAN TRUST

      Dozens of Certified Specialists, One Standard of Care

      Our clinical team includes Certified Prosthetists (CP), Certified Orthotists (CO), and dual-credentialed Certified Prosthetist-Orthotists (CPO), along with certified fitters and pedorthists, working in clinics across the country.

      WHAT OUR TEAM BRINGS

      Care Built Around You

      Every clinician on our team is committed to understanding your goals and building a plan around them, not the other way around.

      Board-certified and state-licensed prosthetists, orthotists, and fitters
      Ongoing clinical education to stay current with the latest technology
      Close collaboration with your physicians and physical therapists
      Continuity of care from evaluation through fitting, adjustment, and follow-up
      Specialized experience in pediatric, adult, and complex-case care
