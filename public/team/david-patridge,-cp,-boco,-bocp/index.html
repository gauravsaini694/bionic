      About David Patridge

      David Patridge joined Bionic in 2025 as a Certified Prosthetist and Orthotist in North Carolina. While serving in the US Navy, he lost his left leg and soon became passionate about the field of prosthetics. I graduated Prosthetic and Orthotics program in Memphis, Tennessee and then moved to North Carolina to practice. He has an immense love for his work, he is very vocal about his compassionate care and to see physically challenged individuals successfully regain mobility. Throughout his 30+ years of experience in his career, he has treated both adult and pediatric patients in Prosthetics and Orthotics and now has proudly joined the Bionic team in 2025. He adores his wife and they spend all of our time together enjoying life in Charlotte and staying active.
