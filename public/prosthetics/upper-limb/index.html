RESTORING FUNCTION

Solutions for Every Level of Upper Limb Loss

From the shoulder to the hand, upper limb prosthetics restore function and independence for daily activities. Thanks to advanced technology, we offer solutions that help you regain control and live life on your terms, whether you need a partial hand or a full arm prosthesis.

LEVELS OF CARE

Prosthetic Options by Level of Amputation

We build upper limb prostheses to match your specific level of loss and your day-to-day needs.

Shoulder (Shoulder Disarticulation / Forequarter) — support for amputations at or above the shoulder joint, with advancing technology options
Above the Elbow (Transhumeral / Elbow Disarticulation) — solutions ranging from passive to computerized systems
Below the Elbow (Transradial) — restoring fine motor skills through a range of component options
Hand & Partial Hand (Wrist Disarticulation / Partial Hand) — lightweight, cosmetic devices that resemble pre-amputation function
Silicone Restoration — replicating the patient's intact limb in fine detail, including wrinkles, hair, freckles, skin tone, and shape
