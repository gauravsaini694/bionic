      WHAT WE OFFER

      Services We Offer

      Every person’s movement is unique, and prosthetic or orthotic solutions should reflect your body, condition, and daily life. At Bionic Prosthetics & Orthotics, we begin with a detailed clinical evaluation to recommend solutions focused on function, comfort, and long-term use.

      Prosthetic Care

      Our prosthetic services support individuals with upper- and lower-limb loss or limb difference at every stage of life.

      We focus on:

      • Restoring balance and natural movement patterns
      • Reducing energy loss and fatigue during walking or daily activities
      • Supporting work, recreation, and independent living

      Each prosthesis is carefully selected and customized based on your anatomy, mobility goals, and lifestyle, with ongoing adjustments as your needs evolve.

      Learn more

      Orthotic Care

      Orthotic care is designed to support the body’s structure, improve alignment, and reduce strain on muscles and joints.

      We provide custom orthotic solutions for:

      • Foot and ankle instability
      • Knee, hip, and spinal conditions
      • Post-injury and post-surgical recovery
      • Long-term musculoskeletal conditions

      Our goal is to improve comfort, stability, and movement efficiency while helping reduce pain and prevent further complications.

      Learn more

      Diabetic Foot Care

      Diabetes can significantly affect foot health, circulation, and sensation, making proper support essential.

      Our diabetic foot care services focus on:

      • Reducing pressure on high-risk areas
      • Protecting sensitive or vulnerable tissue
      • Supporting safe, confident walking

      We design specialized footwear and orthotic solutions to help lower the risk of ulcers and other complications while supporting long-term mobility.

      Learn more

      Pediatric Prosthetics & Orthotics

      Children’s prosthetic and orthotic needs change as they grow, develop, and become more active.

      Our pediatric care emphasizes:

      • Comfort and proper fit during growth
      • Supporting healthy movement and development
      • Encouraging confidence at home, school, and play

      We work closely with families and care teams to ensure each solution adapts over time, supporting both physical development and independence.

      Learn more
